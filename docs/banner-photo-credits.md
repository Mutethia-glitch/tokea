# Tokea banner photography notes

Banner photographs replace decorative icon banners in the native Discover cards. The app uses real drink and bar photography. Some spirit categories intentionally share representative photos rather than falsely claiming every glass is the specific spirit.

- Unsplash photographic assets: https://unsplash.com/license — free use under Unsplash's standard license, photographers retain ownership. The images are loaded from images.unsplash.com with narrow crops.
- Traditional **Muratina** banner: Wikimedia Commons, [“Mratina” drink served in a calabash](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%22Mratina%22_drink_served_in_a_calabash.jpg). Verify the specific photograph’s author/attribution and license terms before any public release or bundling. Do not substitute an unrelated drink photo while claiming it is Muratina.
- Image URLs are presentation-only and unrelated to private ID/selfie review files. No user photo or ID is sent to a banner provider.

**Pre-release:** confirm every remote image loads on actual devices, keep a reliable offline fallback, check credit/licensing and consider bundling optimized approved photos within the native build so banners do not rely on third-party CDNs.
